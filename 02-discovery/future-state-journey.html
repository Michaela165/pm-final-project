Riverty card: future-state journey

Sample data, illustrative, not Riverty research. References (T-xxx, N-xx) point to the fictional data pack. Internal states and the manual workaround are inference.

Persona: A heavy BNPL user who runs two or more pay later apps and checks them before payday.

Goal: See in one place what they owe in total, and when, before payday.

Strategy: One place to see and pay everything owed, in-store as well as online, so users stop checking several apps or falling back on their debit card.

  1. 1

    Get set up

    User action
    Adds card to phone wallet from approval screen → pays in-store same day.
    Internal state
    Feels cautious about one more app → reassured by simple setup.
    Pain point addressed
    Fixing wallet failure (T-102) and ID retries (T-106) → fewer users give up.
  2. 2

    Pay anywhere

    User action
    Taps card in shop or online → pays without reaching for debit card.
    Internal state
    Feels confident, no second thoughts → uses the card by habit.
    Pain point addressed
    Silent declines (T-101, N-06) → user stays with card, not debit.
  3. 3

    See it all

    User action
    Opens one screen with card and pay later totals → sees total owed.
    Internal state
    Feels in control → stops checking other apps.
    Pain point addressed
    Two screens, two totals (T-103, N-07) → one trusted number.
  4. 4

    Settle up

    User action
    Gets one reminder with one amount and due date → pays on time without doubt.
    Internal state
    Feels relieved → trusts Riverty as the place to manage payments.
    Pain point addressed
    Two reminders, different amounts (T-104) → no confusion over what is due.

3 competitive advantages over the manual workaround

  1. One in-app total replaces app-hopping and copying → faster, trusted answer before payday.
  2. Declines explained in the app → user retries instead of falling back to debit.
  3. One due date and one reminder → no reconciling conflicting amounts by hand.
Risk to flag early: Stages 3 and 4 need card and pay later to share one balance and one due date (B3, B8). That is a platform and regulatory dependency, and N-11 says approvals take months. Confirm feasibility with engineering and risk before committing.
